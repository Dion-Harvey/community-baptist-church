Welcome Home

You Belong Here

For generations, Community Baptist Church of West Covina has been a place where families grow in faith, neighbors become friends, and everyone is greeted with a warm smile and an open heart.

We are a Bible-believing Baptist congregation devoted to worship, prayer, fellowship, and service. Whether you have walked with the Lord your whole life or are just beginning your journey, there is a seat — and a family — waiting for you.

Come as you are. Leave encouraged, uplifted, and loved.

Our Mission

To glorify God by sharing the Gospel of Jesus Christ, nurturing believers in the Word, and serving our community with compassion and love.
